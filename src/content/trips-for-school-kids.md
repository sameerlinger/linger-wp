Take the kids to explore the natural wonderland at Bush Camp, Kalpavalli. This is a community restored 9000 acre grassland, where the abundance of life has returned - blackbucks, birding, even the odd leopard around!   
  
The team there are budding naturalists who have even published their own book on the flora and fauna around. A session on the regeneration, birding, hikes and a visit to the Timbaktu Collective for an exposure to regenerative farming and local economies.

Kalpavalli is near Penukonda - about 150kms and 3 hours from Bangalore. The accommodations are comfortable sleeping arrangements on a plinth in a semi open thatched tent setup, with a feel of the outdoors. The staff is local, very knowledgeable and the whole experience is perfectly safe for the students.

Costs vary depending on the group size from 1450 per night to 2250. If you need additional help with transport for the last mile (it's a slightly rough road so vehicles with good ground clearance are recommended), this can be arranged at an extra cost. Please reach out at stay@linger.in with details of the number of students, dates and other requirements, for this.
