# Stays in Chikmagalur and Sakleshpur

The Western Ghats of Chikmagalur and Sakleshpur are coffee country. Linger's stays here are large estates rather than resorts: **Balur Estate**, 400 acres of coffee heritage dating back to 1840, and **Varshini Estate**, a hilltop home on 40 acres of varied terrain.

Come for the quiet, the walks through the plantation and the views.
