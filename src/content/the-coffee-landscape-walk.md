This isn't just a "plantation tour". Of course we'll share some details about the growing of coffee itself, but more importantly, how it connects with a region that's a UNESCO World Heritage Site for its biodiversity, and the catchment of major, life sustaining rivers. 

Lunch at 1pm, a walk around the estate, to the Cauvery, through paddy fields. Then coffee, and conversation around any more questions you might have.
