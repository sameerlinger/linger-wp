We'd love for you to stay with us for long durations. We consider anything over 7 nights a long stay, and happy to work out long term pricing for the same.

- At the farms, this is largely for volunteering and learning opportunities priced at between 1000/- per person per night to about 1750/- per person per night depending on various factors.
- At the estates, the pricing varies between 1450/- to about 3250/- per person per night.

*For pricing for shorter stays, please contact us at **stay@linger.in***
