# Homestays in Coorg

Coorg (Kodagu) is at its best slowly: mornings in a coffee estate, birdsong in the forest, long lunches and nothing much planned. Linger's homestays in Coorg are small, quiet and deliberately low-density, and each one is looked after by a local team who know the place.

Stay on a working coffee estate at **The Linger Farm** in Chettimani, hear the forest from every side at **T Stop** in Theralu, or watch for birds at **Three Wooded Acres**, right next to Madikeri. Whichever you choose, expect home-cooked local food, walks, and very little noise.

Not sure which Coorg homestay suits your group? Send us your dates below any stay and we will help you pick.
