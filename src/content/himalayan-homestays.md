# Himalayan Homestays

For longer, slower holidays, Linger has homestays in the Himalayas: in the hills of Kumaon, on the unexplored side of Corbett in Uttarakhand, and at the foot of the Dhauladhar range in Palampur, Himachal Pradesh.

These are small, private places, well away from the crowds. They suit families, writers and anyone looking to switch off. Long stays and workations are welcome.
