# Farm Stays near Bangalore

A real weekend away does not need a long journey. These farm stays and homestays near Bangalore are anywhere from about an hour to a few hours' drive, and each is a working farm, forest or grassland where you can unwind, walk, and eat food grown nearby.

Nearest are the organic farms at **Doddaballapura** and **Nelamangala**, and the wild hills around **Magadi and Savanadurga**. Go further for the forested slopes of **Melagiri**, the grasslands of **Kalpavalli** or the permaculture farm beside the **Bandipur** forest.

All are quiet, pet-friendly and low-density, which is the point.
