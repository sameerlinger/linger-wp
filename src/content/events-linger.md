**3Bs&1H : Biodiversity & Nature Exploration at The Tamarind Valley Collective**

Come spend a night at Tamarind Valley Collective a.k.a. TVC and experience the farm from dusk to dawn - watching birds, butterflies, bees, insects and herps, exploring trails, and discovering the incredible life at TVC!

What’s in store?

•⁠ ⁠⁠Guided biodiversity walks

•⁠ ⁠⁠Birds, butterfly, bees, insects, herps spotting & documentation

•⁠ ⁠⁠Moth Screen + night walk

•⁠ ⁠Citizen science through iNaturalist

•⁠ ⁠Connect with the TVC community

•⁠ ⁠Farm-produce gifts to take home
