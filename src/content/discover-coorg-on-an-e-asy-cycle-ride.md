A 2-3 hour ride that starts from The Linger Farm, Chattimani, explores the slopes around Bhagamandala (2 options from the Temple Ride and the Forest Ride), with a coffee and snack break in between. 

You ride up and the down Kodagu's slopes with gorgeous views around, almost no traffic, and lovely weather to accompany you. We tell you stories about the place, we stop for Instaworthy pics, and we breathe in the really clean air deep into our lungs!

The ride's easy thanks to e-assist bikes with 7 gears AND 5 levels of assist. There's 4 of them and we need a minimum of 2 people to do this. 

The pricing is 1250 a head for 3-4 people, and 1450 a head for 2. For guests at TLF, this is at 950 and 1150. Al of this payable at the property, to the local entrepreneur who runs this.

Cycles, helmets, support, coffee & snacks all provided. Someone rides along with you, either on the cycles itself or on a bike. 

Come and ride the Western Ghats - the best way to experience it!
