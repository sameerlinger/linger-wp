It’s a given that you should be drinking lots of water a day.

Especially given air-conditioned offices, it becomes even more important to remember to drink – and have a bottle handy that’s easy to refill and keep sipping from. And from what we’ve seen, everyone uses _plastic_ bottles! We don’t like those at all.

Why? Read [this](http://www.onegreenplanet.org/animalsandnature/whats-the-problem-with-plastic-bottles/). Or take a look at [this](http://kids.nationalgeographic.co.in/kids/stories/spacescience/water-bottle-pollution/). Clearly, even recycling is NOT a solution. It has to be stopped at the source of the demand – consumption!

So we decided to do something about it. You’ve already seen we’ve been using these at our properties. They’re pretty popular too – many a guest has asked if they could take one home 🙂

[![Bottled Water - Linger Style](/images/blog/replace-plastic-water-bottles-recycle-environment/P1160128-225x300.jpg)](https://linger.in/wp-content/uploads/P1160128.jpg)
