We’re now live at 3 very do-nothing, quiet locations across the country. And with this, we have our first properties in Kerala and the Himalayas now live.

_**Vanghat, Marchula, in the Corbett Buffer Zone**_

  [![Forests all around](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/Forests-300x199.jpg) ![Bridge across the Ramganga](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/Bridge-300x200.jpg) ![Setting](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/Setting-300x200.jpg)](https://www.facebook.com/media/set/?set=a.10152081777039576.1073741831.325401659575&type=3)

At the end of a 2 km journey on foot and a raft across the river is this seriously committed-to-forests property that respects its surroundings even while making it accessible to us. Run by a naturalist and a team that’s friendly – both with the guests as well as the forests. You’ll love the place, and learn a lot, we assure you!

_**Chestnut Grove, Vijaypur (Uttarakhand)**_

  [![DSC00035](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/DSC00035-300x225.jpg) ![OLYMPUS DIGITAL CAMERA](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/Nanda-Devi-Peaks-300x225.jpg) ![IMG\_0751](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/IMG_0751-300x200.jpg)](https://www.facebook.com/media/set/?set=a.10152076116434576.1073741830.325401659575&type=3)

[6](https://linger.in/wp-content/uploads/IMG_0751.jpg)750ft high up in a very quiet, relatively unknown part of the Himalayas is Vijaypur (or Bijepur) a small little tea town with pine and oak forests, tea estates, and old temple or two and miles of opportunity for walks, hikes and treks. Or of course, do nothing but read a book, sip on tea and enjoy the stunning views of the snow clad mountains in the distance.

**_The Palathra Heritage, Mararikula, Allepey (Kerala)_**

  [![MainHouse](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/MainHouse-300x159.jpg) ![MararikulamLocation](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/MararikulamLocation-300x141.jpg) ![2013-12-10 07.42.49](/images/blog/the-himalayas-wildlife-and-kerala-linger-grows/2013-12-10-07.42.49-225x300.jpg)](https://www.facebook.com/media/set/?set=a.10152087883924576.1073741832.325401659575&type=3)

Amidst the very green, laidback Mararikulam village is an unassuming little space where a very passionate family has resettled old, traditional houses in a backwaters setting. _Illam, Eatti, Chandanam, Maavu_ and _Plaavu –_ the names themselves tell a story of a different time in a context that attempts to keep up with the same even as the world around changes rapidly. The beach is a short walk/ride away, as is the pretty Vembanad lake and the famed Kerala backwaters. What a trip!
