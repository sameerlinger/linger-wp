Everyone’s publishing listicles to help you travel better, smarter, etc. So we thought we should to.

Here’s our list

1.  Do nothing.
2.  _Seriously_, stop planning so much. You’ll figure it out once there.
3.  Go with the flow. Yeah, you don’t _have_ to do anything specific.
4.  Nope, no must-dos, must-sees, must-trys. It’s a _vacation!_
5.  Yes, do absolutely nothing. Sit back and relax and take it as it comes.
