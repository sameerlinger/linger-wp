The [Tour of Nilgiris is India’s Original Bike Tour](http://tourofnilgiris.com/), and still one of the best, toughest, funnest and best known rides.

[![TFNEd7](/images/blog/linger-tour-of-nilgiris-tfn-team-outing/TFNEd7-300x300.jpg)](https://linger.in/wp-content/uploads/TFNEd7.jpg)

Cycling is a fun activity at our locations. And for this, and for the awesome event it is, Linger was proud to [partner](http://tourofnilgiris.com/index.php/partners) with TFN Edition 7 as a prize sponsor, and as a host for the volunteers team. These guys are the backbone of this awesome event, and do this completely selflessly.

The TFN team had [headed over to the Balur Estate](https://www.facebook.com/TourofNilgiris/timeline/story?ut=43&wstart=0&wend=1427871599&hash=-6881252223518021731&pagefilter=3) for a quick offsite last weekend, and they had a ball there!

[![TFNBalurMarch2015](/images/blog/linger-tour-of-nilgiris-tfn-team-outing/TFNBalurMarch2015.png)](https://linger.in/wp-content/uploads/TFNBalurMarch2015.png)

Team TFN, we’re delighted to have been associated, and honored to have hosted the kickass volunteers team. Look forward to more such over the coming years. Keep riding, and have fun!
